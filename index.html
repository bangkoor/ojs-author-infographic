OJS Author Infographic

Turn the Articles report exported from your OJS into an animated “authors at a glance” section for your journal homepage: author countries on a world map, collaboration, and frequent topics.

Your file stays on your device.The CSV is read and processed in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored, and the generated code contains only totals, never names or emails.

1Load your OJS export

Drop the CSV here or click to choose a file OJS → Statistics → Reports → Articles Report (CSV)

2Choose the articles

Used in the source note under the infographic.

Corrections, errata and re-uploaded old manuscripts are left out by default.

3Check author countries

Change a country to correct it. “Conflict” means the Country field disagrees with the country written in the affiliation.

4Pick the topics

Tick up to 18 keywords. Give two keywords the same label to merge them (e.g. “tourism” and “ecotourism”).
